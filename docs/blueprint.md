# **App Name**: Pawsi AI

## Core Features:

- AI Predictive Alerts Tool: A decision-support tool that uses machine learning to identify high-density fauna areas and suggests castration schedules to meet 2030 sustainability goals.
- Stellar Ledger Transparency: Integrates with the Stellar blockchain to provide a verifiable, public log of every donation and municipal license fee.
- Geographic GIS Heatmaps: Dynamic mapping interface visualizing animal census and urban density for municipal governance and resource allocation.
- Verified Animal Health Registry: Immutable digital health passports for animals including clinical history and identity verification via Arkiv Network.
- Adoption & Sponsorship Hub: Municipal-specific marketplace allowing citizens to adopt or sponsor street animals via decentralized micropayments.
- SDG Sustainability Reporting: Analytics dashboard monitoring real-time impact on Health & Wellbeing and Sustainable Cities metrics.

## Style Guidelines:

- The design uses a Light scheme for clarity and professionalism. Primary color is a professional Science Blue (#2563eb), used for data accents and action buttons. The background is a very light, desaturated variant of the blue hue (#f8fafc) to provide a clean, modern canvas. The accent color is a Deep Azure (#0369a1), chosen from the analogous wheel to provide hierarchy and contrast.
- A sophisticated font pairing is used: 'Space Grotesk' for bold, technical headlines and 'Inter' for body text to ensure maximum readability for analytical reports.
- A library of precision line icons from 'Lucide-React' provides visual clarity for data points and navigation.
- The layout is inspired by high-end financial dashboards, utilizing a structured card grid with clear spatial groupings and a collapsible municipal sidebar.
- Smooth, low-latency micro-interactions for map filtering and subtle pulse effects on predictive AI alert nodes.
